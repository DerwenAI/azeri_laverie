## legal fallout

Danske Bank pleaded guilty to US DOJ fraud charges in Dec 2022 and paid ~$2B fines/settlements across US and EU authorities. Two Lithuanian facilitators connected to the scheme, Irene Ellert and Arūnas Mačėnas, were sentenced in Feb 2024 to 9 and 7 years respectively.
